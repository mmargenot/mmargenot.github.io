Writing is thinking, so I write constantly, either professionally or for my own storytelling ends and I think that it's important to keep track of your influences. Here I collect books or series (mostly fiction, some philosophy) that I enjoy.

- *The Beautiful Thing That Awaits Us All*, by Laird Barron

- *Post Office*, by Charles Bukowski

- *The Myth of Sisyphus*, by Albert Camus

- *Piranesi*, by Susanna Clarke

- *The Library at Mount Char*, by Scott Hawkins

- *The Moon is a Harsh Mistress*, by Robert Heinlein

- *A Lush and Seething Hell*, by John Hornor Jacobs

- *The Wheel of Time*, by Robert Jordan

- *The Fisherman*, by John Langan

- *The Raven Tower*, by Ann Leckie

- *The Disposessed*, by Ursula K Le Guin

- *The Medium is the Massage*, by Marshall McLuhan

- *Perdido Street Station*, by China Mieville

- *Too Like the Lightning*, by Ada Palmer

- *God Bless You, Mister Rosewater*, by Kurt Vonnegut
